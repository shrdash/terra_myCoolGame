🛰️ PROJECT AETHEL // MISSION DIRECTIVE

Commander: You are stationed aboard Outpost 84 in geostationary orbit above Terra Prime.

Your objective is complete planetary terraforming before Sol 100.

VICTORY THRESHOLDS:

  • Temperature: Reach at least +14.0°C (Melt glaciers, allow liquid rain)
  • Atmospheric Pressure: Reach at least 980.0 hPa (Earth normal ~1013 hPa)
  • Atmospheric Oxygen: Reach at least 19.5% O₂ (Breathable air without masks)
  • Hydrosphere: At least 800 ML of liquid oceans
  • Colony Population: Expand colony to at least 5,000 citizens

RULES OF SURVIVAL:

  • Never let Net Energy drop below 0: Power blackouts slash terraforming efficiency by 75% and cause colonist casualties.
  • Geothermal Drills [GD] can only be built over Thermal Fissures (**).
  • Ice Harvesters [IH] can only be placed on Glaciers (❄❄).
  • Magnetic Shields [MS] must be placed on Mountain Ridges (^^) to deflect solar flares.

🏗️ Construction Protocol

To deploy structures on the planetary surface:

  1. Click any cell on the 16x16 grid to select it (or use arrow keys).
  2. Click one of the facility buttons in the ribbon toolbar (or press hotkeys 1-8).
  3. Your Research Credits (RC) will be deducted, and the facility code will be written to the cell.
  4. Click ▶ ADVANCE SOL to process the environmental cycle!
